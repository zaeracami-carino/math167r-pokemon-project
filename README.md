Pokemon Data Set: 1025 Pokemon–https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/sarahtaha/1025-pokemon 

For our project, we will be analyzing the Kaggle dataset “1025 Pokemon Stats,” which contains information about Pokemon from the National Pokedex. We will examine Name, National Pokedex Number, Primary Typing, Generation, Legendary Status, Evolution Stage, Height, Weight, and Health, Attack, Defense, Special Attack, Special Defense, and Speed. Name and National Pokedex Number will be used solely for identification.

The question we are trying to answer is: Which physical characteristics, battle statistics, and classifications are associated with higher Pokemon health? This dataset is appropriate because it includes multiple continuous variables, including Height, Weight, Health, Attack, Defense, Special Attack, Special Defense, and Speed. It also contains categorical variables such as primary typing, generation, legendary status, and evolution stage.

We will begin by checking for missing values, inconsistent entries, and incorrect data types. If missing continuous values are present, we will use stochastic regression imputation. We will calculate descriptive statistics, including the mean, median, standard deviation, minimum, maximum, and quartiles. Frequency tables will summarize the categorical variables. Histograms will display the distributions of the continuous variables, while bar graphs and boxplots will compare the Pokemon across different primary types, generations, legendary classifications, and evolution stages. Scatterplots will show relationships between Health and other quantitative variables.

For our advanced statistical analysis, we will be using a multiple linear regression model with Health as the response variable. The explanatory variables will include Attack, Defense, Special Attack, Special Defense, Speed, Height, Weight, Primary Typing, Generation, Legendary Status, and Evolution Stage. This analysis will allow us to determine which battle statistics, physical characteristics, and Pokemon classifications are significantly associated with a Pokemon’s health. We will interpret the regression coefficients, p-values, confidence intervals, and R2 value. We will also examine diagnostic plots and check for multicollinearity to determine whether the model assumptions are reasonably satisfied.

Overall, this project will help us identify patterns in Pokemon health and determine whether stronger battle statistics, larger physical characteristics, legendary status, or specific types and generations are associated with higher Health.
